Who is protected by what

RiskProtection
One relayer offline or wrongThreshold: the others still agree
One relayer signs a false withdrawalNot enough signatures; proof removes it and takes its bond
A group of relayers colludesVault cap, 2,000 a day, 24-hour wait, pause by anyone honest
A seller crashes the priceDaily band: at most −5 % a day
The bridge must closeWithdrawals stay open; each user moves their own funds

Full specification and reviews: github.com/CreditsCommunity/bridge, branch docs/spec

No design removes all trust: if enough relayers collude they can take up to the vault cap. Everything else in the design is about making that cap small, the theft slow and visible, and the cost high.
