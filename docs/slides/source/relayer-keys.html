Two wallets, one on each chain

Credits · the node wallet

Holds: the CS bond (100,000 for founders), a little CS for fees, the 1M CS of delegations it receives.

Pays rewards and the bond back to a safe Credits address chosen with the BSC wallet.

BSC · the BSC wallet, kept offline

Holds: a few cents of BNB and the USDT bond (50 for founders).

Signs: the link to the node, the safe Credits address, and the decision to leave.

The software adds its own signing key, with no funds. Whoever breaks into the server cannot leave, cannot move the bond and cannot redirect the rewards.

The node wallet must be the one receiving the delegations, so the contract sees bond and delegations together; but it lives on the server. For that reason the destination of the bond and of the rewards, and the decision to leave, need a signature from the BSC wallet, which stays offline. A thief with the server keys can at most get the relayer slashed; a successful appeal returns the bond to the safe address. The BSC wallet must be a normal wallet, not a contract, and losing its key loses the USDT bond.
