Joining once the bridge is running

Joining

Bond: worth at least 1,000 USDT or 10 % of the vault, whichever is more; at least half in CS, at least 50 USDT in the vault.

7-day wait, then active automatically once enough relayers confirm the delegations. Nobody can object.

Refused only if its CS would leave the vault cap uncovered.

Staying

Inactive, bond kept: below 50 % of the work in 14 days, or delegations under 1M for 30 days. Back to active once fixed.

A bond fixed at entry never rises later.

Rewards: an equal share of the hourly CS buyback, 20 % to the owner, 80 % to the delegators.

The entry bond grows with the vault, so a bigger bridge asks more of those who join it. A relayer that falls inactive keeps its bond and comes back when the cause is fixed.
