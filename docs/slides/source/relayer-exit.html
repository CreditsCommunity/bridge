Leaving: getting the bond back

Day 0

The relayer calls “leave” on Credits, signed also by its BSC wallet, and stops approving and signing.

Hours

The other relayers update the vault’s signer list: its BSC key stops counting.

30 days

Waiting time: any false signature it made can still be proven. Then the CS come back to its wallet, and the USDT with the relayers’ signatures.

60 days

If the signatures never came, the relayer takes its USDT back alone, with no one’s permission.

The CS go to the safe Credits address the BSC wallet signed, the USDT to the BSC wallet: nobody can redirect them.

The CS part is released by the Credits contract itself after the 30 days. The USDT part is released on BSC: normally with the signatures of the remaining relayers, which their software gives automatically; if they are withheld, after 60 days the vault pays it without signatures. The only exception is a proven false signature: then the bond goes to the reserve, with 30 days to appeal.
