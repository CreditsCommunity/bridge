Three pieces, no administrator

BSC

UsdtVault

Holds the users’ USDT. Pays a withdrawal only with enough relayer signatures.

In between

Relayers

Credits full nodes run by different people. They read both chains and agree on what happened.

Credits

CsUsdtBridge

One contract: the csUSDT token, the CS order book, bonds, rewards and votes.

Nothing moves unless a threshold of relayers agrees: half of them plus one, at most 7. No single key can mint or pay.

The vault on BSC and the contract on Credits have no owner and cannot be upgraded. The relayers are the only link: each reads both chains on its own and the contracts count how many agree.
