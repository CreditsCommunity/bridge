How the relayers are paid

Fees

0.05 per deposit or withdrawal, 0.1 % per trade

Hourly buyback

Each hour 1/168 of the week’s fund buys CS from the cheapest offers

Split weekly

Equal shares to relayers that did ≥ 80 % of the work

Each share

20 % to the node owner, 80 % to its delegators

In scenario A the fund holds 0.36 csUSDT: it buys about 18 CS, about 6 CS per relayer. More traffic, proportionally more. Every fee turns into demand for CS.

Spreading the buyback over 168 hourly slices means that nobody can grab the whole fund with one badly priced offer at the right moment. The CS bought go to the relayers and, through them, to the people who delegated 1M CS to their nodes.
