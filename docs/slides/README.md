# Slides: how the csUSDT bridge works

Open `index.html` in a browser: 23 slides that follow USDT, csUSDT and CS through the bridge,
how one becomes a relayer, and two worked scenarios (3 and 7 relayers). The same numbers are in
[§16 of the specification](../spec.md#16-worked-scenarios).

- `index.html`: all slides on one page, scaled to the window (no build step; fonts from Google
  Fonts). Speaker notes are in the sources.
- `source/`: one HTML section per slide, with the speaker notes in `<aside>`; `deck.json` gives
  their order.

The slides summarise the specification; where they differ, the specification wins.
